Method

What p-means changes

e-means updates centroids with a mini-batch moving average. o-means adds a two-level index over the centroids. p-means keeps that index and changes how the GPU work and the final centroid update are done.

Closing mean
At every training budget, the final centroid is the ratio of an EMA of sums to an EMA of counts. The H100 recipes use a half-pass window.
GPU kernels
Wider scan tiles, grouped accumulation and a cell rebuild without the host read-back reduce work per pass.
Per-dataset recipes
Batch size and the number of cells to probe were swept on seed 10. Seed 10 also contributes to the reported results.

How the benchmark was run

Flash-KMeans and e-means use the paper’s H100 reference numbers. o-means uses its defaults and p-means uses per-dataset recipes, measured on one NVIDIA H100 80GB. Each seed’s speedup is the Flash-KMeans@25 time divided by the time at the first tested budget that reaches its held-out MSE.

The 1M results use ten seeds. The 10M results use three, including the tuning seed. The paper uses ten throughout. One e-means rerun on DEEP* 10M took 0.519 s here, compared with about 0.569 s implied by the paper’s summary.
