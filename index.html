About

I am a Master of Computer Science student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Fan Lai, and expect to graduate in December 2026. I transitioned from the Ph.D. program to the MCS program after two years of graduate research. My work focuses on the systems side of AI: serving and scheduling for agentic and compound AI applications, GPU infrastructure, and distributed systems. I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from Tsinghua University.
